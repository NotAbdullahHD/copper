privacy

Copper is a small start page. Short version: your stuff stays in your browser.

Saved in your browser only

Shortcuts, notes, settings, pinned tabs, recent sites, saved manga and reading progress live in your browser's local storage. Copper has no accounts and no server database.

Analytics

If you click “ok” on the banner, Copper uses Google Analytics to count visits and which engine or game gets used. It is never sent the sites you open, your searches or your notes. Choose “no thanks” (or turn it off in settings) and nothing is loaded.

Browsing through Copper

Pages can load through Copper's proxy (a Cloudflare Worker, or the full proxies on a server). Copper itself keeps no log of what you open, but Cloudflare and the sites you visit see their own normal request data. Don't type passwords or card numbers into sites opened through any proxy.

Other services Copper talks to

  • Google, to fetch site icons
  • OnlineGames.io, for the games catalog
  • MangaDex, for manga (data and images are theirs)
  • Your chosen search engine, when you search
