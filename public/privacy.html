privacy

Copper is a small start page. Short version: your stuff stays in your browser.

Saved in your browser only

Shortcuts, notes, settings, pinned tabs, recent sites, saved manga and reading progress live in your browser's local storage. Copper has no Copper account, and no database of its own.

Analytics

Copper uses Google Analytics and Google Tag Manager to count visits and which engine or game gets used. It is never sent the sites you open, your searches or your notes. Turn “count my visits” off in settings → general and neither loads on the start page. This privacy page loads no analytics at all.

Browsing through Copper

Pages can load through Copper's proxy (a Cloudflare Worker, or the full proxies on a server). Copper itself keeps no log of what you open, but Cloudflare and the sites you visit see their own normal request data. Don't type passwords or card numbers into sites opened through any proxy.

Other services Copper talks to

  • Google, to fetch site icons
  • GitHub's githack and jsDelivr caches, for the game pages and their artwork (ShadowDevLabs/files)
  • MangaDex, for manga (data and images are theirs)
  • TMDB, for movie details
  • our movies api, which resolves the stream and relays the video, when you play a film
  • Your chosen search engine, when you search
  • Supabase, only if you use chat: your chat sign-in and the messages you send are stored there
